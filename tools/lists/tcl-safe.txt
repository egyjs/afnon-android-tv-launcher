# TCL Google TV / Android TV: safe to disable. Telemetry, ads, promos, demos and helpers nobody opens.
# Measured by UrTV on three TCL sets (Android 11, 12, 14): about 85-215 MB more free RAM, no feature lost.
# Apply: bash tools/disable.sh tools/lists/tcl-safe.txt     Undo: bash tools/restore.sh (or tools/enable.sh <pkg>)
# Packages missing on a given TV are skipped. From UrTV (github.com/tshi-dev/UrTV, MIT).
